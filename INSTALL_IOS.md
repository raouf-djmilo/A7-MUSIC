# 🍏 iOS Installation Guide — A7-MUSIC

Because Apple requires apps outside the App Store to be sideloaded, this guide explains how to install **A7-MUSIC.ipa** on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 15.0 or later without jailbreaking.

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the Latest IPA

1. Go to the [A7-MUSIC Releases Page](https://github.com/raouf-djmilo/A7-MUSIC/releases) or click:
   - 👉 **[Download A7-MUSIC.ipa](https://github.com/raouf-djmilo/A7-MUSIC/releases/latest/download/A7-MUSIC.ipa)**
2. Save the .ipa file to your PC, Mac, or iPhone Files.

---

## 🛠️ Step 2: Choose Your Sideloading Tool

Select any of the following popular methods:

### Method A: Sideloadly (Recommended for Windows & Mac)
*Quickest and easiest drag-and-drop tool.*

1. Download and install [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io/) on your PC or Mac.
2. Connect your iPhone via USB cable to your computer and unlock the screen (tap **Trust This Computer** if asked).
3. Open Sideloadly.
4. Drag and drop the downloaded A7-MUSIC.ipa into the IPA icon box in Sideloadly.
5. Enter your Apple ID (used strictly by Apple to sign the personal development certificate).
6. Click **Start** and wait 1–2 minutes until Sideloadly displays **Done**.

---

### Method B: AltStore (Wireless Sideloading)
1. Install [AltServer](https://altstore.io/) on your computer and install AltStore to your iPhone.
2. Transfer or download A7-MUSIC.ipa onto your iPhone (e.g., via AirDrop, iCloud Drive, or Safari).
3. Open **AltStore** on your iPhone, go to **My Apps**, tap the **+** icon in the top corner, and select A7-MUSIC.ipa.
4. AltStore will sign and install the app.

---

### Method C: TrollStore / Scarlet (On-Device)
*If your iOS device supports TrollStore or Scarlet:*
1. Open the downloaded A7-MUSIC.ipa in TrollStore or the Scarlet app.
2. Tap **Install**.

---

## 🔐 Step 3: Trust the Developer Certificate (Essential)

Before opening A7-MUSIC for the first time on your iPhone:

1. On your iPhone, open **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **General > VPN & Device Management** (or *Device Management*).
3. Under **Developer App**, tap on your Apple ID / Developer profile.
4. Tap **Trust "[Your Apple ID]"** and confirm by tapping **Trust**.

---

## 🛡️ Step 4: Enable Developer Mode (iOS 16, 17, 18+)

If your device is running iOS 16 or later:
1. Open **Settings > Privacy & Security**.
2. Scroll to the bottom and select **Developer Mode**.
3. Toggle Developer Mode to **On**.
4. Restart your iPhone when prompted, then tap **Turn On** after rebooting.

---

## 🚀 Step 5: Launch & Enjoy A7-MUSIC!

You can now open A7-MUSIC directly from your home screen.
- Enjoy background playback, dynamic lock screen controls, and live synchronized listening!
