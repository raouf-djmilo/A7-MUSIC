# 🤖 Android Installation Guide — A7-MUSIC

This guide walks you through downloading, installing, and configuring **A7-MUSIC** on any Android device running Android 8.0 (API 26) or newer.

---

## 📥 Step 1: Download the Latest APK

1. Go to the [A7-MUSIC Releases Page](https://github.com/raouf-djmilo/A7-MUSIC/releases) or click:
   - 👉 **[Download A7-MUSIC.apk](https://github.com/raouf-djmilo/A7-MUSIC/releases/latest/download/A7-MUSIC.apk)**
2. Save the .apk file to your device's **Downloads** folder.

---

## ⚙️ Step 2: Enable "Install Unknown Apps"

Because A7-MUSIC is distributed directly via GitHub rather than the Google Play Store, Android requires one-time permission to install from your browser or file manager:

### On Android 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15+:
1. Once the download finishes, tap on the completed download notification or find A7-MUSIC.apk in your **Files / Downloads** app.
2. If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings**.
   - Toggle on **Allow from this source**.
   - Tap the **Back** button and press **Install**.

### On Samsung Galaxy (One UI):
1. Go to **Settings > Security and privacy > Install unknown apps**.
2. Select your browser (e.g., Chrome, Samsung Internet) or **My Files**.
3. Toggle the switch to **On**.

### On Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (MIUI / HyperOS):
1. Go to **Settings > Privacy protection > Special permissions > Install unknown apps**.
2. Select the app you used to download the file and allow it.
3. If a 10-second security verification prompt appears, check the box and tap **OK**.

---

## 🛡️ Step 3: Google Play Protect Notice

If Google Play Protect displays a dialogue stating *"Unrecognized App"* or *"Blocked by Play Protect"*:
1. Tap **More details** (or *Install anyway*).
2. Tap **Install anyway**.
> **Note**: This warning is standard for apps distributed directly outside the Google Play Store that have not been submitted for Google Play store review. The app is completely safe.

---

## 🔋 Step 4: Recommended Battery Optimization Settings

For uninterrupted, crystal-clear background audio streaming:
1. Long-press the **A7-MUSIC** app icon on your home screen and tap the **(i)** Info icon (App Info).
2. Tap **Battery**.
3. Select **Unrestricted** (or disable "Put app to sleep").
4. Ensure **Notifications** are allowed so playback controls remain visible on your lock screen and notification bar.

---

## 🔄 Updating A7-MUSIC

When an updated version of A7-MUSIC is released:
1. Download the new .apk file from the [Releases Page](https://github.com/raouf-djmilo/A7-MUSIC/releases).
2. Install it directly over the existing app.
3. Your playlists, favorites, and settings will remain preserved.
