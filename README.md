## 🌟 Highlights & Key Features

### 🎧 Studio-Grade Audio Playback
- **Persistent Background Engine**: Uninterrupted audio playback with lock screen media controls, headphone button integration, and notification playback tiles.
- **Dynamic Equalizer & Low Latency**: Native audio pipeline engineered for smooth, rich acoustic reproduction.
- **Offline Cache**: Keep your favorite tracks and recent queues ready for playback even without an internet connection.

### 🔮 Apple Liquid Glass UI
- **Translucent Glassmorphism**: Tailored frosted glass interfaces, real-time background blurs, adaptive accent lighting based on album art, and micro-haptic feedback.
- **Fluid Gesture Controls**: Swipe down to dismiss full player, pull-to-refresh feeds, dynamic interactive bottom sheets, and interactive volume sliders.

### 👥 "Listen Together" Synchronized Sessions
- **Live Collaborative Listening**: Stream tracks in real-time lockstep with friends. When you play, pause, or skip, everyone hears the transition synchronously.
- **Social Audio Presence**: See what friends and favorite artists are listening to in real time.

### 📱 Immersive Full Player & Nouble Mini Player
- **Expandable Mini Player**: Continuous playback bar docked above navigation that fluidly expands into a full-screen canvas.
- **Real-Time Visualizer & Lyrics**: Dynamic reactive audio spectrum waveforms and time-synced lyrics display.

### 📸 Stories, Camera Studio & Social Feed
- **Music-Powered Stories**: Capture moments with built-in camera filters, attach soundtrack snippets, and broadcast 24-hour stories.
- **Community Feed & Chat**: Share favorite tracks, create discovery posts, comment, and communicate through secure direct messaging.
